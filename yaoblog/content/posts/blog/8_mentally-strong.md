今天中午听《忽左忽右》请经济学家洪灏谈SVB的破产，遂去看他的[Twitter](https://twitter.com/haohong_cfa?lang=zh-Hant)，发现下面的quote，存下来。

|  |                                            |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| <center>1</center> | They move on. They don't waste time feeling sorry for themselves.      |
| <center>2</center> | They embrace change. They welcome challenges.                          |
| <center>3</center> | They stay happy. They don't waste energy on things they can't control. |
| <center>4</center> | They are kind, fair, and unafraid to speak up.                         |
| <center>5</center> | They are willing to take calculated risks.                             |
| <center>6</center> | They celebrate other people's success. They don't resent that success. |
