Today, I found the anchor piece for my annual essay: Anthropic’s *Scenarios for our Economic Future*.

While the shift has been palpable for some time, this article served as a stark wake-up call—underscoring that knowledge-work roles like mine sit squarely in the crosshairs of exponential AI advancement.

Provoked by this realization, I mapped out how the strategy ops function is likely to evolve across three distinct phases: the **manual era**, the **AI-assisted era**, and the **AI-led era**.

Below is a projection of what this role will look like in the AI-led paradigm—likely by 2030.

## Strategy Ops Role

The following is a text version of the role map shown in the original note:

| Stage | Work covered |
| --- | --- |
| Goal Setting | Metric-factor breakdown; problem diagnosis; factor prioritization |
| Data Insight | Attribution analysis; hypothesis generation |
| Strategy Design | Strategy drafting; experiment design; decision / sign-off |
| Execution | Securing resources; team coordination; plan execution; execution monitoring |
| Review | Impact evaluation; ROI accounting; iteration decision |
| Capability | Methodology codification; first-principle abstraction; monitoring system build |

## New Tasks

- Data & knowledge structuring
- Prompt / skill orchestration
- AI output QC
